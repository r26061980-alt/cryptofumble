Where does the January 2020 price for Ethereum come from?
It's the same method the main CryptoFumble calculator uses for any date older than ~365 days: log-interpolation between known market data points (a workaround for CoinGecko's free-tier limit, which won't return history past one year back). It's an estimate, not that day's exact exchange quote.
